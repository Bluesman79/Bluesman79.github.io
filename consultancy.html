Medical AI technical due diligence

I can support investment teams evaluating medical AI, SaMD, digital health, biomarker, clinical decision-support, and on-device AI companies. The goal is to independently assess whether the company's AI claims are technically credible, clinically meaningful, reproducible, bias-aware, and supported by evidence that matches the intended use.

Typical checks include data provenance, validation design, patient/site leakage, external validation, calibration, subgroup performance, explainability, deployment assumptions, regulatory-facing evidence gaps, and the realism of the startup's claimed performance.
