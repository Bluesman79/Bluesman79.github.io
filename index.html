Research profile

My work focuses on trustworthy and data-centric AI across tabular, imaging, video, text, and multimodal biomedical datasets. I develop reproducible machine learning pipelines for diagnosis, prognosis, biomarker discovery, clinical decision support, and biometric recognition.

Core methodological interests include robust low-data learning, explainable AI, multimodal learning, feature selection, foundation models, LLMs, synthetic data generation, and bias-aware model validation.

Selected strengths

  • Explainable AI and feature selection for biomedical discovery
  • Compact and robust vision transformers for medical imaging
  • Multimodal learning with clinical, imaging, anthropometric, and omics data
  • Model validation, bootstrap confidence intervals, survival analysis, and bias assessment
  • Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, HuggingFace, LangChain
