Current research identity

Trustworthy biomedical AI across imaging, tabular data, omics, and biometrics

My work combines methodological machine learning with clinical and biomedical applications: compact vision transformers, shallow-deep neural networks, explainable feature selection, robust low-data learning, multimodal biomedical data integration, clinical decision support, and secure biometric recognition.

Research profile

I focus on trustworthy, reproducible, and data-centric AI for high-stakes biomedical and biometric systems. My research spans tabular, imaging, video, text, omics, questionnaire, and multimodal datasets.

I develop machine learning systems for diagnosis, prognosis, biomarker discovery, clinical decision support, privacy-preserving biometric recognition, and robust medical imaging.

Methodological contributions

ZACH-ViT

Compact vision transformer research line for efficient and robust medical imaging, including zero-token vision transformer variants, ShuffleStrides Data Augmentation, and robustness evaluation under low-data, corruption, and adversarial stress settings.

ZACH-ViT GitHub

Shallow-deep neural networks (s-DNNs)

Compact feed-forward neural-network methodology for low-dimensional structured biomedical prediction, especially when sample size is limited and conventional clinical scores may not capture nonlinear signal.

Machine-Learning-Enhanced Non-Invasive Tests (MLE-NITs)

Concept introduced for enhancing conventional non-invasive clinical tests with machine learning, including MASLD fibrosis applications comparing s-DNNs, FIB-4, tabular foundation models, and large language models.

Explainable feature-selection methodology

Alternative XAI-oriented methodology for feature selection and biomedical knowledge retrieval, applied to high-dimensional biomedical data such as gene-expression and omics studies.
