Core theme

Democratizing trustworthy biomedical AI

Compact, explainable, train-from-scratch AI for low-data medicine - when data are limited, labels are expensive, and massive GPU workflows are not realistic.

Independent AI/ML medical model audit

From SaaS clinical AI to on-device medical AI

I provide scientific and technical review of medical AI/ML systems before clinical, regulatory-facing, investor due diligence, or production use. The focus is on evidence quality: data provenance, intended use, leakage, confounding, validation design, calibration, subgroup performance, reproducibility, explainability, deployment assumptions, and lifecycle risks.

I also support angel investors, VC funds, family offices, and corporate innovation teams with independent technical due diligence on medical AI, SaMD, digital health, biomarker, LLM/CDSS, and on-device AI startups.

Professional profile

I am a biomedical AI researcher and consultant working across academia, clinical research, and industry, with more than 10 years of production AI experience in the biomedical domain. My work focuses on compact, explainable, and auditable AI for high-stakes biomedical and biometric systems - from low-data medical imaging and biomarker discovery to diagnostic/prognostic tests, MLE-NITs, and privacy-aware biometric security.

  • Production biomedical AI: more than 10 years of experience building, validating, auditing, and translating AI pipelines for biomedical data, including biomarkers, diagnostic tests, prognostic models, ultrasound, imaging, and clinical decision support.
  • Current roles: Postdoctoral Researcher in AI & Biometrics Security at BioML Lab / CODE Research Institute, Universität der Bundeswehr München, and Affiliated Researcher / Sr. AI Data Scientist at Amsterdam UMC.
  • Biomedical AI experience: clinical tabular data, omics, ultrasound, medical imaging, LLM-based decision support, biomarker discovery, and clinical model validation.
  • Industry experience: production-oriented ML/DL projects in healthcare, ultrasound, routing, computer vision, and applied AI consulting.
