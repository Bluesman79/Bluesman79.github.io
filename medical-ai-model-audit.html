For investors and technical due diligence teams

Independent review of medical AI startup claims

I support angel investors, venture capital funds, family offices, corporate innovation teams, and strategic partners with independent technical due diligence on medical AI, SaMD, digital health, biomarker, clinical decision-support, and on-device AI companies.

The review focuses on whether the startup's AI claims are scientifically credible, reproducible, clinically meaningful, bias-aware, and aligned with the claimed intended use. This may include assessment of validation design, data leakage, external validation, subgroup performance, calibration, explainability, deployment assumptions, regulatory-facing evidence gaps, and whether reported performance is likely to generalize beyond the development dataset.

What I audit

  • Intended use, target population, clinical workflow, and claim consistency.
  • Data provenance, cohort construction, inclusion/exclusion criteria, label quality, and missingness.
  • Train/validation/test split design, site-level separation, temporal leakage, patient leakage, and preprocessing leakage.
  • Confounding, batch effects, spectrum bias, subgroup performance, and fairness-relevant failure modes.
  • Metrics, thresholds, calibration, confidence intervals, decision curves, sensitivity/specificity trade-offs, and uncertainty.
  • External validation strategy, distribution shift, robustness, monitoring assumptions, and model lifecycle risks.
  • Explainability, feature stability, biological/clinical plausibility, and biomarker interpretability.
  • Reproducibility of notebooks, code, random seeds, environment, data transformations, and reporting.
